# SoundCloud Toolkit — UI/UX Audit (2026-07-08)

Stack confirmed: Next.js 15 (app router, static export) + React 18 + TypeScript + Tailwind (HSL CSS-variable tokens in `globals.css`, mapped in `tailwind.config.ts`) + custom shadcn-style primitives in `frontend-UI/src/components/ui/`. No Radix. Fonts: Space Grotesk (display) + Plus Jakarta Sans (body). Verified against the live site with desktop (1440px) and mobile (390px) screenshots.

---

## A. Overall impression

The product is **structurally solid but visually noisy and inconsistently finished**. The app shell, primitives (Button/Card/Input/EmptyState/PageHeader/PageContainer), and flagship flows (Combine) are genuinely good — clear hierarchy, sensible empty/loading/error states, token-based styling. Trust erodes in three places:

1. **The landing page stacks ten separate animation gadgets** (FlickeringGrid, Meteors, ShimmerButton, WordRotate, TypingAnimation, AnimatedShinyText, AnimatedGradientText, ShineBorder, GlareHover, TextAnimate). Individually fine; together they read as template/AI-generated rather than a product someone sweats over. The scroll-triggered character animations also leave section headings **invisible in static render** (confirmed in the full-page screenshot — the Benefits h2 doesn't paint).
2. **~330 hardcoded hex color classes across 22 files** (`text-[#333333]`, `text-[#666666]`, `bg-[#FF5500]`, `hover:bg-[#E64D00]`…). Most carry a `dark:` twin so dark mode works, but the light theme is frozen outside the token system — and in `AppShell.tsx` the grouped sidebar links have **no dark variants at all** (real dark-mode bug).
3. **Small trust leaks**: heavy grey smear-shadow under the light-mode glass nav, gradient headline text that fades to near-invisible pale yellow on the cream background, "Merge. your music library." reading as broken grammar, three different names for the same auth action (Login / Connect with SoundCloud / Continue with SoundCloud).

Verdict: 7/10 app, 5/10 landing. Nothing needs a ground-up redesign; it needs discipline.

## B. Top 10 highest-impact issues

1. **Animation overload on landing** — `app/page.tsx` uses 10 animation components; hero + every card wrapped in ShineBorder+GlareHover. Reads templated, hurts perf (multiple infinite animations, canvas). Fix: keep 2 signatures (FlickeringGrid hero + shimmer CTA), remove the rest. **High**
2. **Headings invisible before scroll / in static render** — `TextAnimate`/`TypingAnimation` render empty h2s until JS+scroll (confirmed in screenshot; also SEO/reduced-motion harm). Fix: plain headings. **High**
3. **Hardcoded hex colors across 22 files (~330 instances)** — breaks theming, causes the AppShell grouped-nav dark-mode bug. Fix: map to tokens (`text-foreground`, `text-muted-foreground`, `bg-primary`, etc.). **High**
4. **Sidebar group links broken in dark mode** — `AppShell.tsx` `SidebarGroup` uses `text-[#555555]`/`text-[#999999]`/`hover bg-black/[0.04]` with no `dark:` variants; grouped tools (most of the nav) are near-unreadable dark-on-dark. **High** (bug)
5. **Glass nav shadow** — `.glass-card` in `globals.css` hardcodes `0 18px 45px rgba(15,23,42,0.65)` — a dark-mode shadow applied in light mode; screenshot shows a dirty grey smear under the pill nav, the first thing a visitor sees. Fix: light-mode-appropriate shadow. **High**
6. **Gradient text contrast** — `.text-gradient` ends at `#ffd28f`; `AnimatedGradientText` uses `#ffc14a`. On the cream background (`45 20% 97%`) the tail of "Toolkit", "total control", and the login headline nearly disappear (WCAG failure on headline text). Fix: end gradients at `#ff8a3d`-ish. **High**
7. **Hero copy under-sells** — "The Ultimate SoundCloud Toolkit" says nothing concrete; the one concrete line is the tiny rotating "Merge. your music library." with broken punctuation. The 9-item `features` array with strong copy is **defined but never rendered** (dead code); the features grid shows only 4 items. Fix: concrete subhead + render full feature grid. **High**
8. **CTA naming inconsistency** — nav "Login", hero "Connect with SoundCloud", login page "Continue with SoundCloud". One action, three names. Fix: standardize funnel (nav: Get started → hero: Connect with SoundCloud → login: Continue with SoundCloud is acceptable as a 2-step funnel; nav should not say "Login" for a signup CTA). **Medium**
9. **Modals lack dialog semantics** — `ConfirmDialog.tsx` and the combine playlist-picker have no `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, Escape handling, or focus management. **Medium**
10. **`<button>` nested inside `<Link>`** — landing hero and CTA section wrap `ShimmerButton` (renders `<button>`) in `<Link>` → invalid HTML, double-focusable. ShimmerButton already supports `as="a" href`. **Medium**

Other bugs found (called out separately): dead `features`/`userTypes` consts in `app/page.tsx`; `LoadingSpinner` hardcodes `border-[#FF5500]`; `SelectionBanner` destructive actions render an orange-pulse red button (mixed semantics, pulse color orange on red body).

## C. Landing page recommendations (`app/page.tsx`)

- **Nav**: fix glass shadow; rename "Login" CTA to "Get started"; keep center links.
- **Hero**: keep headline + FlickeringGrid (signature); fix gradient end-stop; replace "Merge. your music library." with a proper subhead sentence stating what the product concretely does (500-track limit, bulk unlike/unfollow, dead-track cleanup, free + OAuth); fix WordRotate grammar or drop; ShimmerButton → `as="a"`.
- **Trust bar**: fine as-is; keep.
- **Features**: render all 9 features from the existing `features` array in a 3-col grid, plain Cards (no ShineBorder/GlareHover); keep badges on the 4 highlighted ones or drop badges. Replace TypingAnimation with static "Built for DJs, curators, and power listeners."
- **Benefits**: replace 8 heavy check-cards with a compact 2-col checklist; plain h2 (remove TextAnimate).
- **How it works**: fine; keep.
- **CTA section**: remove Meteors; keep copy.
- **FAQ / footer**: fine; keep.

## D. Web app recommendations

- **AppShell**: tokenize all sidebar colors (fixes dark bug); keep collapse/hover behavior (good).
- **Dashboard**: 18 flat tool cards is a wall; group by the same categories as the sidebar (Playlists / Social & Activity / Discovery / Links / Utilities) with section headers; keep search across all. Keep stats + quick resolver (both good).
- **Combine** (flagship): already strong. Add dialog semantics to the picker modal.
- **Tool pages with hex classes** (downloads, likes-to-playlist, following-library, genre-search, repost-manager, etc.): tokenize; behavior unchanged.
- **ConfirmDialog**: role/aria/ESC/autofocus.
- **SelectionBanner**: align destructive semantics (red pulse for destructive actions).
- **Login page**: strong already; only the gradient-contrast fix applies.

## E. Design system cleanup

- Replace hex pairs: `text-[#333333] dark:text-foreground` → `text-foreground`; `text-[#666666] dark:text-muted-foreground` → `text-muted-foreground`; `text-[#999999] dark:text-muted-foreground` → `text-muted-foreground/80`; `bg-[#FF5500]`→`bg-primary`; `hover:bg-[#E64D00]`→`hover:bg-primary/90`; `text-[#FF5500]`→`text-primary`; `border-[#FF5500]`→`border-primary`; `bg-[#F2F2F2] dark:bg-background`→`bg-background`.
- `LoadingSpinner`: `border-primary`.
- `.glass-card`: split light/dark shadows.
- `.text-gradient`: `#ff5500 → #ff8a3d` (keep hue, fix contrast).
- Remove landing dead consts (`features` becomes used; `userTypes` removed).

## F. Mobile responsiveness

Confirmed at 390px: layout holds. Issues: trust-bar cards wrap "No password storage" awkwardly (minor); SelectionBanner (fixed bottom) can cover last list rows — pages using it should reserve bottom padding; hero CTAs stack fine. No blocking mobile bugs found.

## G. Accessibility

- Gradient-text contrast failures (headlines) — fixed by gradient change.
- Modals: no `role="dialog"`/`aria-modal`/ESC/focus trap.
- Scroll-triggered text animations ignore `prefers-reduced-motion` and hide content without JS.
- Button-in-anchor nesting (hero CTAs).
- Sidebar dark-mode contrast bug (grouped links).
- Positives: `focus-visible` rings on Button/Input, `aria-pressed` on TrackRow, `aria-label`s on icon buttons, `role="status"` spinner, semantic details/summary FAQ.

## H. Quick wins (Phase 1)

Glass shadow, gradient stops, sidebar dark fix, LoadingSpinner token, CTA naming, WordRotate grammar, ShimmerButton `as="a"`, ConfirmDialog a11y, remove dead consts.

## I. Larger redesign opportunities

Landing rework (animation diet + full feature grid + concrete subhead); dashboard grouping; app-wide tokenization; real product screenshot in hero (needs an authenticated capture — future); genuine social proof (testimonials) to back the "2,000+" claim.
